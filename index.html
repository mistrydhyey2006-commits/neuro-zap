Free IQ Test Online — Measure Your Intelligence in 20 Minutes

Neuro Zap is a free online IQ test designed to give you a fast, accurate estimate of your intelligence quotient. With 20 carefully crafted questions covering five core cognitive areas, you get a real IQ score — not a flattering number designed to make you feel good. No signup required, no payment, no catch.

What Does This IQ Test Measure?

Unlike basic quiz-style tests, Neuro Zap tests five distinct types of intelligence that are used in standardised IQ assessments worldwide:

  • Pattern Recognition — identifying rules in number sequences and visual series
  • Spatial Reasoning — mentally rotating shapes, folding nets, and visualising 3D objects
  • Abstract Logic — deducing conclusions from logical statements and analogies
  • Working Memory — retaining and manipulating information across multiple steps
  • Matrix Reasoning — completing number grids and visual matrices using hidden rules

Together these five areas give a balanced, well-rounded measurement of general cognitive ability — the same skills tested by professional IQ assessments like the WAIS and Raven's Progressive Matrices.

How the IQ Score Is Calculated

Your score out of 20 is mapped onto the standard IQ scale, where 100 is the global average. Scores below 85 suggest below-average performance on this test, scores between 85 and 115 fall in the average range, scores above 115 indicate above-average intelligence, and scores above 130 place you in the gifted range. The scale tops out at 145 for a perfect score.

This is an estimation based on your performance, not a clinically certified assessment. For a certified IQ score, you would need a supervised test administered by a psychologist. However, Neuro Zap gives you a reliable benchmark that correlates closely with how you would perform on a formal test.
